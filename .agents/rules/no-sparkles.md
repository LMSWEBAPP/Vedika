# No Sparkles Icon Rule

## Guideline
**NEVER** use sparkle icons (✨, <Sparkles /> from Lucide or any other icon library) anywhere in the website, UI components, badges, or buttons.

## Recommended Semantic Alternatives
- AI / Tutor: <Bot />, <Brain />, or <Cpu />
- Explanations / Insights: <Lightbulb />, <HelpCircle />, or <BookOpen />
- Questions / Discussion: <MessageSquare /> or <MessageCircle />
- Actions / Execution: <Play />, <Terminal />, or <ArrowRight />
- Bookmarks / Notes: <Bookmark /> or <PenTool />
